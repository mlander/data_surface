Resource, structure, shape

A resource is a thing with identity and storage that operations read and write, so it is the one of the three a caller reaches on its own. A structure is a group of keys only ever held by something else: it has no permission of its own, but it may answer access for its keys. A shape is one named value declared once and used at many keys, each of which may only narrow it.
