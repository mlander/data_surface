Who may ask, what may they see

An operation's permission decides whether you may ask; the access answers decide what you may see and change once you have asked: open, locked or left out, per key, from the resource's access class, a structure's own and each alter, the stricter standing. An answer is static when it reads the account alone, so it is stated ahead for that account, and runtime otherwise, marked live and settled once the thing is known.
