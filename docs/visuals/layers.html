Layered, then sealed

The owner declares its keys and rules; other modules contribute at their own mounts and may narrow the owner's keys but never widen them; refinement narrows, stating static answers and marking live ones; and access locks keys or leaves them out for the account. The build seals the result, and every door reads that one sealed data surface and nothing else. They agree because every contribution is made under its own name before the seal, and after the seal nothing widens.
